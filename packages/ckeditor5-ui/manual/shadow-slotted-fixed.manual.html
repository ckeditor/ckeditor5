Editor slotted into a fixed element of a shadow tree

<ck-manual-fixed-frame> renders its <slot> inside a modal <dialog>, a popover, or a position: fixed panel of its shadow tree. The editor and its overlay container (config.ui.overlayContainer) are its light DOM children, so the balloons render inside that element too. The browser reports no offsetParent for content slotted into a fixed element, while it is laid out against that element.

What to do

  1. Scroll the page down a bit, then open the frame with each of the buttons below the header. The editor shows up once the frame is open.
  2. Select a word to open the balloon toolbar, put the caret in the link to open the link balloon, hover the toolbar buttons to show their tooltips, and use the block toolbar handle.

Expected: the balloons, tooltips, and the block toolbar handle are placed next to what they point at, not offset by the position of the frame or by the page scroll.
