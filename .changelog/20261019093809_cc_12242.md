Restored support for the legacy `--ck-color-focus-border` and `--ck-drop-shadow` CSS variables. Buttons, inputs, the editing area, and menu bar items use `--ck-color-focus-border` for their focus border again. Dropdowns, balloons, and menus use `--ck-drop-shadow` for their shadow again.

Overriding `--ck-interactive-focus-ring` now also changes the focus border of the source editing area. The pulse around the buttons that insert a paragraph next to a widget now matches the refreshed focus color.
