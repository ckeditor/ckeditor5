Block objects, such as tables or media, inserted with a collapsed selection into a block with soft breaks (lines created with <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>, for example, in a list item) are now placed next to the line with the selection instead of before or after the whole block. The object goes after the line when the selection is at the end of the line, and before the line otherwise. Empty first and last lines of the block are kept.

This changes the result of `model.insertObject()` with the `findOptimalPosition: 'auto'` option.
