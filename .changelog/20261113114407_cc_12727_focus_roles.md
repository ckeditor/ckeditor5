The focus styles of the accessibility help dialog and of focused widgets read the semantic focus roles (`--ck-interactive-focus-ring` and `--ck-interactive-focus-shadow`) instead of the foundation focus tokens, so overriding these roles also changes them. The "Powered by" and evaluation badges placed on the editor border take the width and style of their border from `--ck-interactive-focus-ring`. The default look does not change.
