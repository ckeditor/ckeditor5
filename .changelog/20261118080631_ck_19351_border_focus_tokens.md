Added theme tokens for the button states: `--ck-button-on-border`, `--ck-button-active-border-color`, `--ck-button-focus-shadow`, `--ck-button-disabled-focus-shadow`, and `--ck-button-focus-outline` with `--ck-button-focus-outline-offset` (the outline shows on keyboard focus only). Added `--ck-list-item-button-border` and `--ck-list-button-on-focus-border-color` for lists, and `--ck-editor-editable-border-color` and `--ck-editor-editable-focus-shadow` for the editing area. The default look does not change.
