The `mergeTableCells` command and the directional merge commands (`mergeTableCellRight`, `mergeTableCellLeft`, `mergeTableCellUp` and `mergeTableCellDown`) are now disabled when the selection contains both header and data cells. Previously, they were reported as enabled in this case, even though such cells cannot be merged.
