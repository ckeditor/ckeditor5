Open list item buttons, for example open menus in the menu bar and in dropdown menus, can be fully themed from `:root` with the new `--ck-list-item-button-open-hover-background` and `--ck-list-item-button-open-text-color` CSS custom properties, next to `--ck-list-item-button-open-background`. The open hover background falls back to the open background, so two properties style the open state with and without the pointer.
