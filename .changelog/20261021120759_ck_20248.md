Pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> while a tooltip of the focused toolbar button is visible now moves the focus back to the editing area with a single key press. Previously, the first press only hid the tooltip. Tooltips displayed on mouse hover can still be dismissed with <kbd>Esc</kbd> without triggering other actions.
