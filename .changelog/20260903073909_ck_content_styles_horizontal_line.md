Horizontal lines now use a lighter background color. As this is a content style, the change also affects already-published content. To restore the previous color, see the [content styles rollback snippets](https://ckeditor.com/docs/ckeditor5/latest/updating/migration-to-refreshed-theme.html#published-content) in the migration guide.
