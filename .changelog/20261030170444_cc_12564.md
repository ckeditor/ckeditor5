The background of the editing area can be themed on `:root` (or `:host` in a shadow root) with the new `--ck-editor-editable-background` CSS custom property. It reaches the main editing roots of every editor type, not nested editables or secondary editors, and is read through the `background` shorthand, so it also accepts gradients and images.
